# Forensic Traceability

Meaningful engineering history must be reconstructable.

Historical records must not be silently rewritten to conceal:
- rejected proposals
- failed implementation attempts
- workflow overrides
- emergency paths
- superseded requirements
- incorrect assumptions
- previous approvals

Corrections should create a new revision, superseding record, or corrective event.


Lifecycle transition decisions must identify the requested transition and supporting or blocking evidence. Refused transitions do not mutate authoritative state. Mandatory checks that cannot execute are recorded as blocked/unavailable, never passed.
