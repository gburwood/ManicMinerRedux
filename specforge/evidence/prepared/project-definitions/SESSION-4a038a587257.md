## Purpose

Establish Manic Miner Redux as a single-player cavern platform game preserving classic Manic Miner gameplay and physics: collect every required item to open the exit, avoid enemies and lethal hazards, and manage limited air and lives; retain deliberate movement, committed jumps, collision, falling, conveyors and crumbling platforms with consistent deterministic rules. Use an 8-bit visual and sound identity with pixel art, limited colours and compact readable screens. Introduce progressive graphical corruption as the player advances, while preserving readable hazards and stable underlying gameplay and physics. Develop Willy growing awareness of his artificial world through escalating contextual reactions and environmental narrative. Provide a level designer to create and edit caverns, place terrain, items, enemies, hazards, spawn and exit, save and load levels and playtest them using the same gameplay rules. This initial change defines project intent only; engine, target platforms, exact physics parameters, level count, narrative script, asset licensing and implementation milestones will be decided through later governed work before their implementation.
